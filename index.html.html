The Story

Built from Ahmedabad, for Ahmedabad

I started Rizqhub in April 2025 with one simple belief — that small and medium businesses in Ahmedabad deserve the same quality of digital work that big corporations pay agencies lakhs for. Growing up in Jodhpur Village, I watched local shops, traders, and service providers struggle to get noticed online, not because their work wasn't good, but because they didn't have the right digital support around them.

Everything at Rizqhub is done personally by me. When you WhatsApp me, you're talking to the same person doing your SEO, cleaning your spreadsheets, or converting your PDFs. There's no middleman, no delays, and no corporate runaround. If you need something at 11 PM because you have a presentation tomorrow morning — I'm available.

My goal is simple: deliver clean, honest digital work at prices that make sense for a local business. I'm not here to impress you with jargon. I'm here to get the job done, on time, every time.
